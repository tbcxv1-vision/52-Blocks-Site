The system

Built on Three Pillars

52 Blocks is not a collection of flashy moves. It is a system — three principles woven together into a defense that never leaves you exposed.

The Guard

A living shield built from framing, angles and elbow lines that protect your center from every direction.

Counter Flow

Turn defense into offense instantly. Every block sets up a counter without breaking rhythm.

Real Pressure

Trained against live, resisting partners so your technique holds up when it actually matters.

What you'll learn

The 52 Hand Blocks

The guard is a language, and these are its words — 21 named defenses that chain together into a shield nothing gets through. You build them level by level.

Level 1

  1. Skull and Crossbones
  2. Raise the Gate
  3. Answer the Phone
  4. Close the Door
  5. Open the Door

Level 2

  1. Roll the Dice
  2. Stack the Deck
  3. Cover the Bullet
  4. Double Pyramids / Rob the Bank
  5. Windshield Wipers

Level 3

  1. Cage / Double Guard
  2. Roof Top
  3. Dirty Birds
  4. L Block
  5. Triple / Triangle Trade

Level 4

  1. Stepladder
  2. Tug the Pants
  3. Full Body Armor
  4. Uptown
  5. Superman
  6. Illusions

Names like Answer the Phone, Roll the Dice, and Windshield Wipers aren't just colorful — each one is a specific frame, angle, and elbow line you can call up under pressure.
