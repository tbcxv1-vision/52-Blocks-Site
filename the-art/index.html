The Art

What Is 52 Blocks?

A close-quarters defensive fighting style built around one idea: a guard so complete that nothing gets through. It is fast, adaptive, and grounded in protecting yourself before anything else.

See How We Train

The core principles

Cover. Flow. Counter.

Everything we teach comes back to these three ideas. Master them and the rest of the art unlocks naturally.

Cover

Your guard is always working. There is no moment you are open.

Flow

Blocks chain together seamlessly so pressure never breaks your structure.

Counter

Every defense hides an answer. Absorb, redirect, then strike back.

The story of the art

Where 52 Blocks Comes From

Three chapters — from where it was forged to how it's taught today.

01Origins

Born From Necessity

The history and evolution of 52 Blocks is rooted in the fighting arts of captured slaves — a system built for someone with no weapons and no room to run. It survived because it worked.

02The System

Blocks Become a Language

Knocking and Kicking. Virginia Scufflin'. Carolina Chaos. Jailhouse Rock. Different names, one idea — a mostly-hands defense where individual blocks link into flowing chains that answer any attack.

03Today

Refined for Real Training

Like Bruce Lee's Jeet Kune Do, it stays a martial art of experimentation. The Federation teaches it with modern coaching, live drilling, and a structured curriculum anyone can follow — beginner to master.

Legacy & recognition

Beyond the Dojo

Prof. Mo calls himself a Renaissance Man — actor, historian, author, stuntman, filmmaker, and SAG-AFTRA producer. The 52 Blocks name has traveled far beyond the mat.

On Screen

Influenced by Ron Van Clief and Fred Williamson, Prof. Mo studied film in college and works today as an actor, stunt performer, and SAG-AFTRA producer — carrying 52 Blocks into film and television.
