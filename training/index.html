The 52 Hand Blocks

Both paths are built on the same core — the named defenses of the 52 Blocks system. Here's the full set, level by level, so you know exactly what you're working toward.

Level 1

  1. Skull and Crossbones
  2. Raise the Gate
  3. Answer the Phone
  4. Close the Door
  5. Open the Door

Level 2

  1. Roll the Dice
  2. Stack the Deck
  3. Cover the Bullet
  4. Double Pyramids / Rob the Bank
  5. Windshield Wipers

Level 3

  1. Cage / Double Guard
  2. Roof Top
  3. Dirty Birds
  4. L Block
  5. Triple / Triangle Trade

Level 4

  1. Stepladder
  2. Tug the Pants
  3. Full Body Armor
  4. Uptown
  5. Superman

Names like Answer the Phone, Roll the Dice, and Windshield Wipers aren't just colorful — each one is a specific frame, angle, and elbow line you can call up under pressure.

Path 2 · Road to Black Belt

The Belt Curriculum

The Road to Black Belt + Master Course is the full in-person and virtual progression. Here's what each stage covers — start where you are, go as far as you want.

01

White → Blue

The fundamentals — move, cover, and hit before anything else.

  • 7 Points & slap boxing
  • Knee / front kick
  • Head movement
  • Footwork
  • Shadow boxing
  • Karate blocks
02

Blue · Green · Purple

The 52 Blocks drill set — chaining the guard under live pressure.

  • Head Tap · Raise the Gate · L Blocks
  • Skull and Crossbones · Close / Open the Door
  • Windshield Wipers · Dem Bows · Stack the Deck
  • Handball Killer · Double Pyramids (Rob the Bank)
  • Triple Trade · Tug the Pants
  • Knocking and kicking · Elbows
03

Brown Belt

Hands-on control — grabs, chokes, and the ground.

  • G-Locks — one/two-hand, cross-hand & collar grabs, bear hugs, head lock
  • Choke defense — one-hand, front & rear
  • Grappling basics
  • Self-defense basics — the sucker punch
04

Black Belt

Weapons — awareness, defense, and application.

  • Weapons overview
  • Stick / bat
  • The ABCs of knife
  • Basic firearms

Beyond Black Belt, the Master Course path continues toward teaching and running your own program.
